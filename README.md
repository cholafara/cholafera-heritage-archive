# CholaFera Heritage Archive

**Preserving the History, Heritage, Currency and Cultural Collections of Bangladesh and the World.**

---

## 🌍 About the Project

**CholaFera Heritage Archive** is a long-term digital preservation project dedicated to collecting, documenting, and presenting the historical, cultural, and numismatic heritage of Bangladesh and the world.

Our mission is to create a freely accessible online archive that preserves valuable historical materials for researchers, students, collectors, educators, museums, and future generations.

---

## 🎯 Project Vision

To become one of the most comprehensive digital heritage archives by preserving and showcasing:

* Historical Coins
* Paper Money (Banknotes)
* Passports & Official Documents
* Historical Artifacts
* Traditional Agriculture & Rural Heritage
* Paintings & Artwork
* Museums & Collections
* National Symbols
* Historical Photography
* Cultural Heritage
