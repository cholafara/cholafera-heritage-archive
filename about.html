About Cholafera Heritage Archive

Preserving History, Heritage, Currency and Cultural Collections for Future Generations.

About Cholafera Heritage Archive

Cholafera Heritage Archive is a digital heritage project dedicated to preserving and sharing the history, heritage, currency, and cultural collections of Bangladesh and around the world.

Our goal is to build a trusted archive where researchers, collectors, students, and history enthusiasts can explore valuable historical information in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is to preserve, document, and share historical coins, banknotes, medals, stamps, and other heritage collections through a modern digital archive that is accessible to everyone.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become one of the leading digital heritage archives, preserving historical collections and making them freely accessible for education, research, and future generations.

Our Objectives

  • Preserve historical heritage for future generations.
  • Build a trusted digital archive.
  • Support education and historical research.
  • Promote the cultural heritage of Bangladesh.
  • Connect collectors, researchers, and history enthusiasts.

What We Collect

  • Historical Coins
  • Banknotes
  • Commemorative Coins
  • Medals & Tokens
  • Postage Stamps
  • Historical Documents
  • Cultural Heritage Objects
  • Digital Archive Collections

Why This Archive

Many historical collections are lost, damaged, or remain inaccessible to the public. Cholafera Heritage Archive was created to preserve these valuable resources in digital form and make them available for everyone.

Our archive helps researchers, students, collectors, and history enthusiasts explore authentic historical information from a single trusted platform.

Md. Mahmudul Hassan

Founder

Cholafera Heritage Archive

Md. Mahmudul Hassan is the Founder of Cholafera Heritage Archive, a digital initiative dedicated to preserving the history, heritage, currency, and cultural collections of Bangladesh.

He actively collects, documents, and archives historical coins, banknotes, medals, stamps, and heritage materials with the vision of making authentic historical resources accessible to researchers, collectors, students, and future generations.

Organization

Cholafera Heritage Archive

Established: 2024

Head Office: Dinajpur, Rangpur Division, Bangladesh

Service Area: Dinajpur, Rangpur Division, Bangladesh

Cholafera Heritage Archive is a Bangladesh-based digital heritage preservation project dedicated to documenting, preserving, and sharing historical coins, banknotes, medals, stamps, documents, and other cultural heritage collections.

Our goal is to build a trusted digital archive for researchers, collectors, students, and heritage enthusiasts while preserving Bangladesh's history for future generations.

Supporting Organization

Cholafera Tour & Travel

Established: 2024

Head Office: Dinajpur, Rangpur Division, Bangladesh

Cholafera Tour & Travel is a Bangladesh-based travel company providing affordable B2B and B2C tour services across Bangladesh.

The organization promotes cultural tourism by encouraging people to explore the historical, archaeological, and heritage destinations of Bangladesh through safe, affordable, and memorable travel experiences.

As the supporting organization of Cholafera Heritage Archive, it helps promote Bangladesh's history, culture, and heritage through responsible tourism and public awareness.
